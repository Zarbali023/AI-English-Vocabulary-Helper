# Starter Vocabulary List

This list is the first test set for the AI English Vocabulary Helper.

## 1. Analyze

- **Part of speech:** verb
- **Meaning:** to examine something carefully in order to understand it
- **Example:** We need to analyze the survey results before making a decision.
- **Practice:** What does it mean to analyze information?

## 2. Clarify

- **Part of speech:** verb
- **Meaning:** to make something easier to understand
- **Example:** Could you clarify what you mean by that sentence?
- **Practice:** Which action clarifies an unclear instruction?

## 3. Consistent

- **Part of speech:** adjective
- **Meaning:** behaving or happening in the same reliable way
- **Example:** Her consistent practice helped her improve.
- **Practice:** What is one sign that a result is consistent?

## 4. Evaluate

- **Part of speech:** verb
- **Meaning:** to judge the quality, value, or usefulness of something
- **Example:** We will evaluate the tool by testing it with several learners.
- **Practice:** Why should you evaluate an AI answer?

## 5. Infer

- **Part of speech:** verb
- **Meaning:** to reach a conclusion from evidence rather than from a direct statement
- **Example:** From the dark clouds, I inferred that rain was likely.
- **Practice:** What evidence could help you infer an answer?

## 6. Modify

- **Part of speech:** verb
- **Meaning:** to change something slightly in order to improve or adapt it
- **Example:** I modified the example sentence to make it clearer.
- **Practice:** How could you modify a difficult explanation?

## 7. Relevant

- **Part of speech:** adjective
- **Meaning:** directly connected to the subject or question
- **Example:** Please include only relevant information in the summary.
- **Practice:** Which detail is relevant to the question?

## 8. Reliable

- **Part of speech:** adjective
- **Meaning:** likely to be accurate, dependable, or trustworthy
- **Example:** We used a reliable dictionary to check the definition.
- **Practice:** What makes a source reliable?

## 9. Verify

- **Part of speech:** verb
- **Meaning:** to check that something is true, accurate, or correct
- **Example:** Always verify an unfamiliar definition before using it.
- **Practice:** What could you do to verify this answer?

## 10. Context

- **Part of speech:** noun
- **Meaning:** the surrounding information that helps explain the meaning of something
- **Example:** The context shows that the word means “a formal request,” not simply “a question.”
- **Practice:** Why can context change a word's meaning?
