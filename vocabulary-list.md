# AI Vocabulary List

Plain-English explanations for common terms used when working with AI.

## 1. Artificial intelligence (AI)

- **Part of speech:** noun
- **Meaning:** computer systems designed to perform tasks that usually require human intelligence, such as understanding language or recognizing patterns
- **Example:** We used artificial intelligence to generate a first draft.
- **Practice:** What kind of tasks can AI help with?

## 2. Prompt

- **Part of speech:** noun
- **Meaning:** the instruction or question given to an AI system
- **Example:** I improved my prompt by explaining the audience and the required format.
- **Practice:** What information can make a prompt clearer?

## 3. Model

- **Part of speech:** noun
- **Meaning:** the trained AI system that produces an answer from an input
- **Example:** The model summarized the article after I provided the text.
- **Practice:** What does a model do with a prompt?

## 4. Context

- **Part of speech:** noun
- **Meaning:** information surrounding a request that helps AI understand what the request means
- **Example:** I included the assignment instructions as context for the AI.
- **Practice:** Why might an AI answer improve when it has more relevant context?

## 5. Token

- **Part of speech:** noun
- **Meaning:** a small piece of text that an AI model processes; it may be a whole word, part of a word, or punctuation
- **Example:** A long document may contain many more tokens than a short question.
- **Practice:** Why does the length of a document matter to an AI model?

## 6. Hallucination

- **Part of speech:** noun
- **Meaning:** an AI-generated statement that sounds confident but is false, unsupported, or invented
- **Example:** The citation was a hallucination because the source did not exist.
- **Practice:** How can you check for an AI hallucination?

## 7. Bias

- **Part of speech:** noun
- **Meaning:** an unfair or one-sided influence that can affect an AI system's output
- **Example:** We looked for bias in the examples before using them for learners.
- **Practice:** Why should people check AI answers for bias?

## 8. Verification

- **Part of speech:** noun
- **Meaning:** the process of checking whether information is accurate and supported
- **Example:** I performed verification by comparing the definition with a reliable dictionary.
- **Practice:** What source could you use for verification?

## 9. Agent

- **Part of speech:** noun
- **Meaning:** an AI system that can plan and carry out several steps toward a goal, sometimes using tools
- **Example:** The agent searched the project files and prepared a draft report.
- **Practice:** Why should you review an agent's actions?

## 10. Fine-tuning

- **Part of speech:** noun
- **Meaning:** additional training that adapts an AI model for a particular task or type of information
- **Example:** Fine-tuning can help a model follow a specialized writing style.
- **Practice:** When might a specialized model be useful?
